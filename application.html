Consent to process personal information

Form A3 · POPIA
Read the full consent

I/we hereby voluntarily consent to the collection, recording, storage, updating, use, disclosure and other lawful processing of my/our personal information by the Apostolic Faith Mission of South Africa Pinkster Park Trust, its duly authorised trustees, management, employees, professional advisers and service providers, to the extent reasonably necessary for the lawful administration of Pinksterpark and the assessment or administration of my/our application.

The personal information processed may include identity particulars, contact details, marital information, address details, financial information, vehicle details, occupancy details, supporting application documents and correspondence reasonably required for the application and management process. Certain limited special personal information, or information relating to criminal matters, may be processed only where lawful, necessary and relevant to community safety, legal compliance, risk management, or the proper exercise of rights and obligations within Pinksterpark.

Personal information may be processed for: verifying identity and eligibility; assessing applications; conducting affordability, credit or background checks where lawfully authorised; administering occupation, cession, leasing and community records; facilitating communication; enforcing the Park Rules, Cession Rules and related governance documents; safeguarding persons and property; complying with legal obligations; and resolving disputes or complaints.

Personal information may be shared, where reasonably necessary and lawful, with trustees, management, professional advisers, credit bureaux, attorneys, auditors, insurers, security providers, maintenance contractors, regulatory bodies or other third parties who require the information for a legitimate and lawful purpose connected to the administration of Pinksterpark. It shall be stored securely and retained only for so long as reasonably necessary or as required by law.

I/we may request access to personal information held about me/us, request correction of inaccurate information, object to certain processing where legally justified, or lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

Please give your consent to continue.

Consent for credit check and debt review enquiry

Form A4
Read the full consent

I/we hereby voluntarily and expressly authorise the Pinkster Park Trust, Park Management, and any duly authorised agent or service provider acting on their behalf, to obtain and assess my/our credit profile and related information for the purpose of considering and administering my/our application. This includes enquiries with any registered credit bureau, credit provider, debt counsellor, employer, bank or other lawful source of relevant financial information, insofar as reasonably necessary to evaluate affordability, payment behaviour, existing credit obligations, debt review status, administration, insolvency, sequestration or any materially relevant financial risk.

This consent extends to a formal credit check and a debt review enquiry. Information obtained will be treated as confidential, processed in accordance with applicable law, and used only for lawful purposes connected with the application and its administration.
